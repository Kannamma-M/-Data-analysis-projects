# 🔎 Borrower’s Risk Insights

## 💰 Loan Amount vs Income
- Wide variation in loan requests relative to income  
- Outliers request disproportionately large loans  

**Interpretation:**  
High loan-to-income mismatches represent higher risk.

---

## 📈 Loan Amount vs Loan Percent of Income
- Most borrowers: 0.1–0.3 ratio  
- Some reach 0.5–0.6 → heavy debt burden  
- Higher ratios → increased rejection likelihood  

**Interpretation:**  
Loan percent of income is a critical risk metric.

---

## 👔 Experience vs Loan Status
- Approved borrowers: ~5–6 years experience  
- Rejected borrowers: ~2–3 years experience  

**Interpretation:**  
Work experience signals career stability and repayment ability.

---

## 🕒 Credit History vs Loan Status
- Longer credit histories (10+ years) → higher approvals  
- Shorter histories (<5 years) → higher rejections  
- Approval likelihood rises steadily with credit history length  

**Interpretation:**  
Credit history length is one of the most decisive factors.

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways
1. Income-to-loan ratio strongly drives rejection.  
2. Work experience boosts approval likelihood.  
3. Longer credit histories improve trust and reduce risk.  
4. Risk hotspots: borrowers with high loan-to-income ratios, short credit histories, and limited work experience.
